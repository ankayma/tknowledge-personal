Docs

T-knowledge is a store for an assistant's knowledge in which every line is a principle, a checked claim (T) or an assumption (A), signed by a named person, with the ids it rests on. It is reached as an MCP server.

Connect

Claude — web, Desktop, mobile

  1. Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. URL: {{MCP_URL}}
  3. When Claude first calls a tool that needs your store, it shows Connect. Enter your email; a six-digit code is sent to that inbox. Paste it in. Your first sign-in opens a store of your own.

Then /mcp, pick tk, and sign in.

What the store holds

KindMeaning
claimA statement about the world that can be checked against a source. Every T and every A is one.
P.nA principle: an axiom, added and retired, never rewritten. A new store starts with seven.
T-nA claim that passed the gate — a fact backed by a document, or a conclusion from at least two standing entries. Its wording never changes.
A-nAn assumption, with what would settle it. Promoted to T when the evidence arrives, refuted when the opposite is observed.
D-nA document: readable text plus a pointer to the original (which stays where you keep it), and how you looked at it. Bound when a URL and hash tie the text to an original, unbound when it is text alone — both are valid evidence.
TH-nA thread: one matter you follow, with keywords, linking the entries, people and conversations that belong to it.
I-nAn instruction: how an assistant should use this store — a meta line (M1), a directive (1, 2 …) or a definition (def:claim). Not a claim and not a principle; added or retired, never edited.
S-nA summary of a thread: always an A, with a named author; each sentence cites ids in the thread; marked stale when newer entries arrive.
conv_nA conversation: a chat or message trail linked to a matter, kept as context — it informs, it is not cited as proof.
F-nA flag: something needs a person — evidence withdrawn, a source that changed between readings. The store raises it instead of resolving it quietly.
attesterA person who stands behind a claim by adding their own document for it.

Tools

The assistant picks these on its own; you talk in plain language.

ToolUse
assume · update_assumptionHold something not yet checked, and revise it while it is open.
document_addRecord evidence as text, with where the original is.
admit · promotePut a checked claim into the ledger; raise an assumption once its evidence is in.
attest · withdraw_evidenceAdd or withdraw a document behind a claim.
demote · retire · recheck · refuteTake down what no longer holds; record that a source was checked again; refute an assumption.
principles · principle_add · principle_retireRead, add or retire principles; retiring one lists everything standing on it.
instructions · instruction_add · instruction_retireHow assistants use your store, kept in the store: one list for every project — meta lines about the list, numbered directives, and definitions of the store's concepts. Added or retired, never edited; wording already there is caught as for claims.
store_infoWho maintains this deployment and where to send feedback when tk misbehaves.
thread_open · thread_tag · link · unlink · thread_closeGroup entries into a matter you follow.
summarizeWrite a reading of a thread — drafted by the assistant, approved by you, every sentence citing ids.
ledger · entry · chain · open_items · thread · lookup · documents · flagsRead: what is known, one entry, what it stands on, what is still open, a thread, search by keyword, documents, warnings.
check_claim · defineAsk whether a sentence is a claim yet, and what the store's words mean. These work before signing in.

Refusals

A refusal is an answer, not an error. It comes with a code, a sentence saying why, and next — the move still open — so the assistant can tell you what to write instead. Common ones:

  • CLAIM_FAILED — not a claim yet: two sentences in one, a relative date, a person not named.
  • NO_SOURCE — a fact names no document, or not where in it the claim is read.
  • SINGLE_PREMISE — a derivation needs at least two premises.
  • PREMISE_NOT_FOUND — a premise id is not in the truth ledger (an assumption is not).
  • USE_PROMOTE — that sentence is already an open assumption; promote it with evidence.
  • SOURCE_NOT_RETRIEVABLE, USE_DOCUMENT — a fact needs a document someone else could open.
  • PREMISE_NOT_T — a conclusion cannot stand on something that has fallen.
  • SUMMARY_UNCITED — a sentence in a summary cites no entry of the thread.
  • CLAIM_TOO_MANY_WORDS, WRITE_LIMIT_REACHED — a store's limits.

Every refusal carries its own reason and next. Ask the assistant to run define on a word of the store — claim, T, A, evidence, promote … — for the store's own explanation.
